# The mouths as estuaries (2026-10-01, D-041; on #110's mouths)

`estuary.png`: before (left, 78f46a0) and now (right): the gentlest mouth (`gentle_sea`,
`--view 2112,4.0,-4785,0,-20`) from 4 m and from 40 m (`--view 2112,44,-4785,0,-45`), and the
largest (`--view -5138,45,-258,118.3,-45`). Under 1.5 m over the sea a river widens to twice its
width at the sea's level and shallows by two fifths, and its banks flatten to three tenths of
their rise (`RibbonParams::estuary`, `ChannelParams::beach`). The mouths flare into the surf
instead of crossing the beach as a canal (ꟻLIP means 0.11, 0.055 and 0.066).

#110's seam (the line across the water where the ribbon ends, seen from 2 m over a mouth) is not
addressed by this; its views are on the old island and are re-placed below when it is.
